
This section contains all of the known Off-Premise Physical Retailers from across the Globe that sell Wine. Off-Premise Physical Retailers sell Wine (and other Alcohol), from a Physical (Brick & Mortar) Location to their Customers, to be consumed off-site. This category of Retailer typically includes Bottle-shops, Supermarkets and so on.

All Off-Premise Physical Retailers in this section are divided up by the country in which they operate.
