This area of the Wiki covers all of the Businesses in the Global Wine Industry, with the exception of [[Wineries/index]]. We divide this area up firstly by **Business Type**...

- [[Businesses/On-Premise Retailers/index|On-Premise Retailers]] - Includes Bars, Pubs and Licensed Restaurants.
- [[Businesses/Wholesalers/index|Wholesalers]] - Includes Importers and Wholesale Distributors.
