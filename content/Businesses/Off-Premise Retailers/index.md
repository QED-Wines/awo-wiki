title: Off-Premise Retailers
---
This section contains all of the known Off-Premise Online Retailers from across the Globe that sell Wine. Off-Premise Online Retailers sell Wine (and other Alcohol), from an Online Website to their Customers, to be consumed off-site (such as at home, upon delivery). This category of Retailer typically includes "Online-Only" Wine Retailers.

All Off-Premise Online Retailers in this section are divided up by the country in which they operate.
