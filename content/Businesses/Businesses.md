
This area of the Wiki covers all of the Businesses in the Global Wine Industry, with the exception of [[Wineries]]. We divide this area up firstly by **Business Type**...

- [[Informational Services]] - Covers Online Magazines, Personal Blogs and so on.
- [[Off-Premise Online Retailers]] - Includes Online Wine Retailers.
- [[Off-Premise Physical Retailers]] - Includes Bottle-shops, Supermarkets, etc.
- [[On-Premise Retailers]] - Includes Bars, Pubs and Licensed Restaurants.
- [[Wholesalers]] - Includes Importers and Wholesale Distributors.
